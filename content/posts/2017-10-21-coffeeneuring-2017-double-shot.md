<div class="separator" style="clear: both; text-align: left;">
<a href="https://chasingmailboxes.com/2017/09/27/seven-is-a-lucky-number-the-7th-annual-coffeeneuring-challenge/">Coffeeneuring</a> ride 2 is in the books. Nate and I rode out to Blue Mound State Park for #coffeeoutside. Next stop was Crossroads Coffeehouse, which also happens to be a <a href="http://www.madisonbikes.org/bike_bingo_2017">Madison Bike Bingo</a> destination. Finished the ride with some more bingo stamps in Middleton. A wonderful day on the bike!</div>
