<p>Today's ride was a bit of a repeat of <a href="http://ride-or-pie.blogspot.com/2017/11/coffeneuring-bike-bingo-and-side-of.html">a 2017 coffeeneuring adventure</a>. The weekend before Halloween means it's time for the Cross Fire cyclocross race in Sun Prairie. Of course 'cross on Halloween means there are costumes involved, and so I figured this would be a good destination. I packed the #coffeeoutside kit, homemade ginger snaps, and my <a href="https://amzn.to/3nGhxOM">Sony A6000</a>.
