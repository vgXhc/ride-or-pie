The <a href="https://chasingmailboxes.com/2018/09/24/coffeeneuring-challenge-2018-the-best-intentions/">2018 Coffeneuring season</a> has started! I usually try to get an early ride in, and so I decided to ride to Hilldale Mall this morning. I generally hate going to malls, even supposedly nicer and more urban ones like Hilldale, but a new branch of <a href="http://crescendomadison.com/">Crescendo Espresso Bar</a> had opened there a couple days ago. I'm a big fan of Crescendo and their pour-overs, and the mall was close enough to squeeze in a ride before work, while also sticking to my theme-within-a-theme: Only get coffee at shops that I haven't been to before, #coffeeoutside in new locations, or doing the "Home Coffee Shop" thing.<br />

<b>Distance:</b> 9.6 km / 6 mi<br />
<b>Coffee:</b> A pour-over with a light roast from Ethiopia (I think <a href="https://anodynecoffee.com/collections/coffee-collection/products/ethiopia-sidama-bensa-arroresa">this one)</a><br />
<b>Weather:</b> Not much above freezing. I'm not ready for that yet.<br />
<b>Other observations:</b> Spotted some turkeys in Shorewood Hills; outdoor music in malls is really horrible; Crescendo staff is still pretty new to all of it; I really need a new front chainring; I look stupid wearing the coffeeneuring bandana<br />
