<div class="separator" style="clear: both; text-align: left;">
<a href="https://chasingmailboxes.com/2017/09/27/seven-is-a-lucky-number-the-7th-annual-coffeeneuring-challenge/">Coffeeneuring </a>season is over, and I have a few ride reports to catch up on. This one is from late October. Madison Bike Bingo was still going on and so I combined getting a stamp from Manna Cafe with seeing a cyclocross race, and checking out a new-to-me coffee shop, What's Brew'N, in Sun Prairie. I also added a few roads to my collection.</div>
