My 2017 coffeeneuring season started slow: Instead of doing a <a href="http://driftlessrandos.org/?page_id=579">200-kilometer brevet</a>, I let the weather forecast change my plans. Heavy rain was forecast all day, and so I snuck out early to ride to Stone Creek Coffee on Madison's east side. I had been to their Milwaukee location before, which is very pretentious but does serve great coffee. The pourover coffee I had here in Madison in contrast was mediocre. I suspect they may not have given it enough time to brew.<br />
24 kilometers total distance, and lots of rain indeed.<br />
