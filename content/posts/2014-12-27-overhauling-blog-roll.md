Blog rolls or link lists on blogs often don't age well, and this blog is no exception. So here are my current regular reads:<br />
<br />
<ul>
<li><a href="http://madisonbikethings.tumblr.com/">MadisonBikeThings</a>: A Tumblr for all things bike in Madison, my home town. Full disclosure: MadisonBikeThings is a 100% subsidiary of the&nbsp;<i>Ride or Pie?! Global Publishing Co.</i></li>
<li><a href="https://groups.google.com/forum/?hl=en#!forum/internet-bob">iBOB Google Group</a>: The Bicycle Owners Bunch is a great resource for all things practical cycling</li>
<li><a href="http://bikesnobnyc.blogspot.com/">BikeSnob NYC</a>: The Bike Snob never ceases to amuse me, even after however many years I've been reading his blog</li>
<li>The <a href="http://wisconsinbikefed.org/blog/">Wisconsin Bike Fed's blog</a> has a great mix of posts on all things Wisconsin cycling, and their main author, Dave Schlabowske, is an excellent photographer and writer.</li>
<li><a href="http://www.aviewfromthecyclepath.com/">A View from the Cycle Path</a>: David Hembrow is an excellent source for teaching the anglophone world how create a great cycling environment. The complementary blog to this is&nbsp;</li>
<li><a href="https://bicycledutch.wordpress.com/">Bicycle Dutch</a>: More video content than &nbsp;Hembrow and a less negative undertone.</li>
<li><a href="http://revolutioncycles.net/">Revolution Cycles</a>: Great local bike shop in Madison</li>
<li><a href="http://janheine.wordpress.com/">Off the Beaten Path</a>: Jan Heine, editor of Bicycle Quarterly, the only bike magazine worth reading, has a great blog, featuring epic (yes, that's the appropriate term here) ride reports and interesting tech stuff</li>
<li><a href="http://www.biketinker.com/">Bike Tinker</a>: Philip is a fellow iBOB, and his blog is a great resource for all kinds of bike-related projects, often appropriate for DIY imitation</li>
<li><a href="http://www.urbanadonia.com/">Urban Adonia</a>: An anthropologist-activist-academic writing about advocacy, equity, and race in bike culture and infrastructure</li>
<li><a href="http://cyclingspokane.blogspot.com/">Cycling Spokane</a>: Kinda like my own blog, but from the West</li>
<li><a href="https://geekguyandy.wordpress.com/">Andy's Cycling Blog</a>: RL friend and randonneur</li>
<li><a href="http://somafab.blogspot.com/">Soma Fab blog</a>: SOMA makes lots of cool bike stuff, as does</li>
<li><a href="http://velo-orange.blogspot.com/">Velo Orange</a></li>
<li><a href="http://bikeportland.org/">BikePortland</a>: I don't live in Portland, but one doesn't have to to appreciate the best local bike journalism there is.</li>
</ul>
<div>
Recommendations for blogs to read are always welcome!</div>
