I just came across this awesome 1897 bike map of Montreal on <a href="http://www.reddit.com/r/montreal/comments/21lwqe/bicycle_map_of_the_island_of_montreal_and/">reddit</a>. Back then, Montreal had not yet encountered the automobile -- only in November 1899 did <a href="http://ici.radio-canada.ca/emissions/a_rebours/2012-2013/chronique.asp?idChronique=258192">Ucal-Henri Dandurand drive the first car in Montreal</a>.&nbsp;As you can see on the upper left of the map, the dotted lines represent "good roads," whereas the solid red lines are just "roads." It would be fun to do an overlay of the present-day bike network and the cycling roads of 1897...<br />
