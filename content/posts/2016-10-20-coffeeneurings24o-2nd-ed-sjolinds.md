<div class="separator" style="clear: both; text-align: left;">
Last weekend offered another opportunity for a combined S24O/<a href="https://chasingmailboxes.com/2016/09/17/6th-annual-coffeeneuring-challenge-the-spirit-of-coffeeneuring/">coffeeneuring</a> adventure. Friends of mine had grand plans: Bike to Blue Mound State Park late on Friday, camp there, ride 200 kilometers the next day, camp again, and then ride back to Madison. My wrist and general fitness were definitely not up for the middle part, but another quick fall S24O camping trip sounded good.&nbsp;</div>
<div class="separator" style="clear: both; text-align: left;">
<br /></div>
<div class="separator" style="clear: both; text-align: left;">
As per usual, I didn't manage to pack my stuff the night before and therefore only left Madison around 5pm. It was a beautiful fall evening, warm enough for 3/4 shorts and a short-sleeve jersey. I was riding Grando, with two <a href="https://www.amazon.com/Ortlieb-Front-Roller-Classic-Bag/dp/B000NWG2DE/ref=as_li_ss_tl?ie=UTF8&amp;qid=1476995651&amp;sr=8-1&amp;keywords=ortlieb+front+roller&amp;linkCode=ll1&amp;tag=riorpi-20&amp;linkId=23e0dc3d182c58642145e1f2de4aa006">Ortlieb Front Roller panniers</a> and my handlebar bag. The sun was slowly setting, and by the time I rode through Mount Horeb, it was mostly dark. My friends, who had left Madison earlier, texted me that they were on the wait list for a table at Hooterville in Blue Mounds, famous for its Friday fish fry. After dinner and a couple beers, we headed to the state park and set up camp. I finally got a chance to try my new hammock, but of course it was pitch dark by now, my headlamp was low on battery, and one of my <a href="https://www.amazon.com/Whoopie-Slings-Hammock-Suspension-Shackles/dp/B01HN1B6DI/ref=as_li_ss_tl?ie=UTF8&amp;qid=1476995906&amp;sr=8-1&amp;keywords=whoopie+sling&amp;linkCode=ll1&amp;tag=riorpi-20&amp;linkId=416f0087bb8d74d14d21a29b9cb51a1d">whoopie slings</a> seemed to have some sort of malfunction. In the end I did get the hammock and tarp set up more or less properly and slept alright. More testing is required to see if I'm a hammock person or not...</div>

&nbsp;The others had set their departure to 6:30am, and since I was up early anyway, I quickly packed up my stuff and got on the road at the same time. While they turned west and then north, I headed back east into Mount Horeb. I was the first customer of the day at <a href="http://www.sjolinds.com/">Sjölinds Chocolate House</a>. While their main attraction certainly are the various kinds of chocolates, sweets, and quiches, they also have decent coffee. I hung out there for a while, reading <a href="https://www.amazon.com/Brethren-Novel-John-Grisham/dp/0345531973/ref=as_li_ss_tl?ie=UTF8&amp;qid=1476996331&amp;sr=8-34&amp;keywords=john+grisham&amp;linkCode=ll1&amp;tag=riorpi-20&amp;linkId=be38a1155c19dcaa79b0f73365d8f095">a John Grisham novel</a> I had picked up from the Little Free Library next to Sjölinds's bike parking, first drinking a cold brew and then a regular coffee.<br />
