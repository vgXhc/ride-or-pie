<p>&nbsp;Yes, this still is a bike blog. But we all know there is <a href="http://ride-or-pie.blogspot.com/search/label/coffeeneuring">some overlap between the bike and the coffee scene</a>. So please allow me to publish a very brief post on fixing my espresso machine.&nbsp;</p><p>I have a Saeco Aroma, bought refurbished in 2013. It's just enough espresso machine for my needs, and it's been working reliably for years. There always had been a little leakage from the steam wand into the drip tray, but only recently the dripping increased to a point where I needed to do something about it.</p><p></p><div class="separator" style="clear: both; text-align: center;"><a href="https://blogger.googleusercontent.com/img/b/R29vZ2xl/AVvXsEi4VFn5ZkbXhI-P4u8c5gpRsATe-1ss7B8lQ_QKlP-Bq3ndW9nGZdcHkHfATbaYcSXAblr_xjWwlPkl3-fdQ2FcnfL8auLv1C2CgGdshwDzNO5jcFPM6rNWmsCBf6K4Yf-NU6UU0CDyZ9I/s4096/IMG_20201121_152502910.jpg" style="margin-left: 1em; margin-right: 1em;"><img border="0" data-original-height="4096" data-original-width="3072" height="400" src="https://blogger.googleusercontent.com/img/b/R29vZ2xl/AVvXsEi4VFn5ZkbXhI-P4u8c5gpRsATe-1ss7B8lQ_QKlP-Bq3ndW9nGZdcHkHfATbaYcSXAblr_xjWwlPkl3-fdQ2FcnfL8auLv1C2CgGdshwDzNO5jcFPM6rNWmsCBf6K4Yf-NU6UU0CDyZ9I/w300-h400/IMG_20201121_152502910.jpg" width="300" /></a></div><br />&nbsp;<p></p><p>I spent a good amount googling around for a solution. Those two parts prevent the knob from being turned all the way out.&nbsp;</li><li>Remove the snap ring. This is where things got a little tricky: Without a visual reference, I wasn't sure how much material to remove.So I took it slow, starting out with extra-fine steel wool. The grooves were still visible when I tested thing the first time, and indeed there was still dripping. I removed a little more material, now using sandpaper. Brass is soft, and so it doesn't take long to remove a lot of material -- be careful, and maybe use a finer grit sandpaper. The second time round it seems like I got it right. There are still a couple drops when pulling a shot, but way less than before.</p><p></p><table align="center" cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0" class="tr-caption-container" style="margin-left: auto; margin-right: auto;"><tbody><tr><td style="text-align: center;"><a href="https://blogger.googleusercontent.com/img/b/R29vZ2xl/AVvXsEhLB28EEn2Sk_s6gZtagr-GOb-Sn1U00zCzdr5CJcFPYZWBvpkTa9qJ3wNkfIbuMpxdPEHdU1DxV5hfmQc03FbITeGMm2K4vUGktnV3mCjdAaG6280tQM5UOaPCqpkTXUmdvKD6Q2OyHL4/s3072/IMG_20201121_131629194_HDR.jpg" style="margin-left: auto; margin-right: auto;"><img border="0" data-original-height="2304" data-original-width="3072" height="300" src="https://blogger.googleusercontent.com/img/b/R29vZ2xl/AVvXsEhLB28EEn2Sk_s6gZtagr-GOb-Sn1U00zCzdr5CJcFPYZWBvpkTa9qJ3wNkfIbuMpxdPEHdU1DxV5hfmQc03FbITeGMm2K4vUGktnV3mCjdAaG6280tQM5UOaPCqpkTXUmdvKD6Q2OyHL4/w400-h300/IMG_20201121_131629194_HDR.jpg" width="400" /></a></td></tr><tr><td class="tr-caption" style="text-align: center;">Pretty smooth now!<br /></td></tr></tbody></table><br />&nbsp;<p></p><p></p><p>Reassembly is easy, except that I couldn't remember which of the two grooves on the know the snap ring went on.</p><p>If you found this post helpful, buy some stuff from Amazon through my affiliate link!
