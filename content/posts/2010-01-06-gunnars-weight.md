I ain't no weight weenie but I nonetheless wanted to add the weight the <a href="http://ride-or-pie.blogspot.com/2010/01/gunnar-is-ready-for-ride.html">previous post about Gunnar</a>. With the addition of Shimano PD-M520 pedals, two bottle cages, and changing the tires to 23mm Michelin Krylions I ended up at about 10.3 kg (measured with bathroom scale). I had hoped to reach the 10 kg mark, but I suppose with a 62cm steel frame that's not easy.
