I love cycling and I love photography. So it's exciting to see that probably the most famous photo agency, Magnum Photos, is releasing a <a href="http://amzn.to/23aq49n">book about cycling</a>. Based on the pictures I've seen on <a href="http://amzn.to/23aq49n">Amazon</a> and <a href="http://www.bjp-online.com/2016/04/magnum-mine-archive-to-launch-celebration-of-the-bicycle/">elsewhere</a>, this looks very promising. Guy Andrews, founder of Rouleur, selected the images from Magnum's vast archives and provides the text. The photographers featured include names such as Henri Cartier-Bresson, Robert Capa, John Vink and Harry Gruyaert. I'll probably preorder the book (release date is June 14) and report back once I have it in hand.
