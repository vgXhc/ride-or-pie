Have you ever been bothered by the plastic hex nuts that <a href="http://www.amazon.com:80ie=UTF8&amp;field-brandtextbin=Ortlieb&amp;node=3375251/?_encoding=UTF8&amp;camp=1789&amp;creative=390957&amp;linkCode=ur2&amp;tag=riorpi-20&amp;linkId=UQWKFTXCA6PV3KWT" target="_blank">Ortlieb</a> uses inside their bags? Quite possibly your answer will be “no” and you haven't ever thought about them. However, some people <i>are</i>&nbsp;bothered by them, as they stick out into the inside of the bag and can get snagged on things—and also look a little cheap.<br />
<br />
When, after many years of heavy use, I lost one of the nubs, I remembered that I had once seen an <a href="http://www.rad-forum.de/topics/912596">alternative on a German bike touring forum</a>. Unfortunately, several different local hardware stores didn't have the required parts. But hey, <a href="http://www.mcmaster.com/">McMaster-Carr</a> to the rescue!<br />

These are the required parts, from left to right: <br />
<br />
<ul>
<li><a href="http://www.mcmaster.com/#92010A222">12mm M4 machine screw</a></li>
<li><a href="http://www.mcmaster.com/#98472A250">Countersunk washer for machine screw</a></li>
<li><a href="http://www.mcmaster.com/#91116A130">12mm OD M4 flat washer</a></li>
<li><a href="http://www.mcmaster.com/#93625A150">M4 nylock nut</a></li>
</ul>
<br />

For a full conversion of a <a href="http://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B00GNIAOIQ/ref=as_li_tl?ie=UTF8&amp;camp=1789&amp;creative=390957&amp;creativeASIN=B00GNIAOIQ&amp;linkCode=as2&amp;tag=riorpi-20&amp;linkId=VXLI24JIPHQ2FGS4">Back Roller</a> you will need thirteen of each. I got everything in stainless steel, which is probably a bit overkill and quite expensive. If you're paranoid about waterproofness, it might make sense to add a little <a href="http://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B004O4JG26/ref=as_li_tl?ie=UTF8&amp;camp=1789&amp;creative=390957&amp;creativeASIN=B004O4JG26&amp;linkCode=as2&amp;tag=riorpi-20&amp;linkId=JKQLU2GTNHTC4GWH">Seam Grip</a> before tightening.</div>
