I made and installed a DIY mudflap, made out of an old shampoo bottle, yesterday. Instructions (in German, but you don't really need them anyway) can be found <a href="http://de-rec-fahrrad.de/technik/spritzlappen">here</a>. Of course, today it was raining, and therefore I can say: they do work. They look a little goofy because they're <a href="http://www.femail.com.au/img/garnier_fructis_sham_cond.jpg">Fructis-green</a> but finding a black shampoo bottle is not exactly easy. Function over form.<br />
