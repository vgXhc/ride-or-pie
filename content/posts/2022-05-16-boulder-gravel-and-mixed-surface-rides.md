To make sure the reassembled bike worked , and also to get a sense how the altitude would affect me, I took a short ride on the classic Flagstaff Road. This is a steep and windy road, offering great views. There is a paved shoulder on some sections. Car traffic was moderate, and people were clearly used to lots of cyclists riding this road.
