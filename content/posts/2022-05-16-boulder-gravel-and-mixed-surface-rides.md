<p><i>I just returned from a week-long vacation in Boulder, Colorado. This was my first time in Boulder (and Colorado), and there was great biking. When researching my ride options I found it difficult to find good riding information in a single place. This series of short blog posts summarizes my rides.&nbsp;</i></p><p>For context: I was on a drop bar bike with 38 (rear) and 42 mm wide tires and a low gear of 32-30. </p><p></p><table align="center" cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0" class="tr-caption-container" style="margin-left: auto; margin-right: auto;"><tbody><tr><td style="text-align: center;"><a href="/images/IMG_20220508_174606132_HDR.jpg" style="margin-left: auto; margin-right: auto;"><img border="0" data-original-height="3072" data-original-width="4096" height="480" src="/images/IMG_20220508_174606132_HDR.jpg" width="640" /></a></td></tr><tr><td class="tr-caption" style="text-align: center;">One of several switchbacks on Flagstaff Rd<br /></td></tr></tbody></table><br />My bike was delayed on the way (thanks, United...). By the time it was delivered to Boulder it was already late in the afternoon. To make sure the reassembled bike worked , and also to get a sense how the altitude would affect me, I took a short ride on the classic Flagstaff Road. This is a steep and windy road, offering great views. There is a paved shoulder on some sections. Car traffic was moderate, and people were clearly used to lots of cyclists riding this road. A wedding party with a photographer was just getting out of their cars there when I arrived. If it's good for wedding photos, it must be good for me as well, I thought, and so I walked a few dozen feet past the end of the pavement. Very much worth it, especially around sunset!</p><table align="center" cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0" class="tr-caption-container" style="margin-left: auto; margin-right: auto;"><tbody><tr><td style="text-align: center;"><a href="/images/IMG_20220508_181149788_HDR.jpg" style="margin-left: auto; margin-right: auto;"><img border="0" data-original-height="3072" data-original-width="4096" height="480" src="/images/IMG_20220508_181149788_HDR.jpg" width="640" /></a></td></tr><tr><td class="tr-caption" style="text-align: center;">Vista from Artist Point<br /></td></tr></tbody></table><p><br />
