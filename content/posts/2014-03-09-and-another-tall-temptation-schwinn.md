After <a href="http://ride-or-pie.blogspot.ca/2014/03/tall-bike-no-not-that-kind.html">the tall Boulder custom</a> I linked to last week I discovered this pretty<a href="http://madison.craigslist.org/bik/4303796348.html"> 63cm Schwinn Voyageur 650B conversion</a> on Craigslist. This is more in my price range and seems like a great opportunity to give the 650B thing a try. The front geometry isn't low-trail (as far as I can tell) but maybe Soma will soon deliver on their <a href="http://somafab.blogspot.ca/2013/05/low-trail-forks.html">promised low trailer conversion fork</a>. I think if this bike hasn't sold by the next time I'm down in Madison the next time won't be able to resist.<br />
