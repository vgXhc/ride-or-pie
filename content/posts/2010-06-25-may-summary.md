It's been ages since I've posted -- too many papers to be written, too much riding, too much traveling. Well, I'll try to catch up a bit in the next few days. First things first, here is the summary of my May riding:<br />
<br />
Wolfgang was ridden for 392 km, Gunnar for 504 km, making a total of 896 km or 28.9 km per day. A clear record month! This makes my accumulated annual riding 2578 km. We'll see if I can top my May riding in any of the remaining months. Neither June nor July are likely candidates, as I just have too much other stuff going on.<br />
