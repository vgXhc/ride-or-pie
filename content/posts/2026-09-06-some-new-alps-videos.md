I promote my videos on a few different social media platforms, but I just remembered that I haven't posted much on here lately. Let's rectify this!

The release frequency is slow and uneven, but there are several new episode about the 2024 Alps trip on my YouTube channel at this point. [Last time I posted about this](https://ride-or-pie.netlify.app/2025/03/videos-from-alps), we had only just gotten into the Alps, on day 2 of the trip. Now we're up to day 8 out of the 11 total days.

# Day 3: The pass not taken<

Day 3 of our bike trip through the Alps. The original plan would have 
taken us up the Kunkelspass. But since Ben's knees were still not great 
and it was raining, riding a steep, partially unpaved pass seemed like a
 bad idea. So we stayed in the Rhine valley before turning toward the 
Albula. And we encounter an unpleasant surprise.

# Day 4: Albula and Bernina

Two named, legit passes separated our start in Filisur and our finish 
near Tirano in Italy: The Albula and Bernina Passes were the two highest
 ones of our whole trip. And maybe the most beautiful ones?
