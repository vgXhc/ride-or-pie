Just a quick update:<br />
<br />
<ul><li>It's still winter. Today the snow has reached us and I might go on a little adventure on the South Hill Recreation Way on my way home from campus.</li>
<li>Forecast for next weekend looks comparatively good (high of -1°C), so maybe I'll be able to go for a ride.</li>
<li><a href="http://ride-or-pie.blogspot.com/2010/01/brooks-got-here.html">The new Brooks</a> feels pretty good so far, but due to the weather I haven't had a chance to test it on a longer ride.</li>
<li>in terms on working on the stable: I installed an Avid Single Digit 7 V-brake on the accomplice's bike in order to increase stopping power and allow installing the BUMM Cyo headlight</li>
<li>upgrade plans: On Wolfgang, I'm going to install a triple front crank as part of a general spring overhaul. In terms of parts and work, this will require something between the ideal of only switching the front derailleur to a triple and&nbsp; the worst case scenario of a new bottom bracket, new front derailleur, new (longer cage) rear derailleur. Most people who have done this conversion on a Cross-Check seem to be switching their bottom bracket from 113mm to 118mm, but maybe it'll work without that. Certainly worth a try.&nbsp;</li>
<li>My <a href="http://ride-or-pie.blogspot.com/2010/02/shameless-self-promotion.html">ebaying efforts</a> haven't been terribly successful: The two Oryx brakes were sold for a whopping total of 2.50$ and two of the saddles didn't sell at all. At least the SDG saddle sold for 19.50$ but I'm still awaiting payment. But whatever -- at least I got rid of some stuff.</li>
<li>Another potential upgrade: I might try buying a used <a href="http://www.zzing.de/English_Zzing/">Zzing USB charger</a> for running my GPS and charging cell phones via the hub generator. There's a good <a href="http://forumslader.de/">DIY solution</a> out there, but if I get a used Zzing it wouldn't save me much money and my soldering skills are a bit rusty.</li>
<li>As you can <a href="http://biketopus.blogspot.com/2010/02/biking-plans.html">see on the accomplice's blog</a>, we've signed up for a <a href="http://flcycling.org/?p=1187">biking Memorial Day weekend</a> in the <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High_Peaks">High Peaks Region</a> of the Adirondacks! It's still far away, but I'm really looking forward to it.&nbsp;</li>
