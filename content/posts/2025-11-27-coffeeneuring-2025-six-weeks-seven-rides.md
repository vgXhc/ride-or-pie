<p>&nbsp;I used to be a regular Coffeeneur (and have the patches to prove it). For a few years, though, I have been on a break. I'm not sure why. Probably a lack of new destinations, riding indoors more? Anyways, this year I returned.&nbsp;</p><h2 style="text-align: left;">Ride 1: Crossroads Coffeehouse</h2><p style="text-align: left;">A mostly overcast fall day took me out to Cross Plains. I'd been to Crossroads Coffeehouse before, but it was a long time ago. It's a very nice coffee shop right on the main street and I had an iced coffee (3/5) and a surprisingly good vegan/gluten free brownie.&nbsp;</p><p style="text-align: left;"><a href="https://www.strava.com/activities/16121545776">65 km / 40 miles&nbsp;</a></p>

<h2 style="text-align: left;">Ride 5: Alice Good in Verona</h2><p>Another ride to Verona, but this time to a shop I'd been to before: Alice Good. I really enjoyed my previous visits, but their limited opening hours had prevented me from going there more frequently. I was glad to find out that their hours are much better now and so I headed there on a Friday afternoon. Again, I loved it: Their staff are very friendly, the espresso was excellent, and I also picked up some excellent light roast beans to take home. On the way back I got rained on a little, but that was fine too.&nbsp;</p><p>&nbsp;</p><div class="separator" style="clear: both; text-align: center;"><a href="/images/8623960a2bdfb48f.jpg" style="margin-left: 1em; margin-right: 1em;"><img alt="" data-original-height="4064" data-original-width="3056" height="640" src="/images/8623960a2bdfb48f.jpg=w480-h640" width="480" /></a></div><br /><p></p><p></p><div class="separator" style="clear: both; text-align: center;"><a href="/images/174919becac515bc.jpg" style="margin-left: 1em; margin-right: 1em;"><img alt="" data-original-height="4064" data-original-width="3056" height="640" src="/images/174919becac515bc.jpg" width="480" />&nbsp;</a></div><div class="separator" style="clear: both; text-align: center;"><div class="separator" style="clear: both; text-align: center;"><a href="/images/7f89517776d9d636.jpg" style="margin-left: 1em; margin-right: 1em;"><img alt="" data-original-height="4063" data-original-width="2286" height="640" src="/images/7f89517776d9d636.jpg=w360-h640" width="360" /></a></div><br />&nbsp;</div><a href="https://www.strava.com/activities/16388471687">45 km / 28 mi</a><p></p><p>&nbsp;<br />&nbsp;</p>

<h2 style="text-align: left;">Ride 6: Fat Cat Coffee Works in New Glarus</h2><p style="text-align: left;">Mid November. But warm enough to wear knickers and sit outside, with a cold brew coffee! I spontaneously took the day off and set out for the longest coffeeneuring expedition of the year: Fat Cat Coffee Works in New Glarus is an old favorite, right off the Badger State Trail. I included some detours and new-to-me roads and it was all glorious. The coffee itself was as usual: Fine but nothing to rave about. But I didn't care in the slightest on a day like that.&nbsp;</p><p style="text-align: left;"></p><div class="separator" style="clear: both; text-align: center;"><a href="/images/0844dd4f3ce31df8.jpg" style="margin-left: 1em; margin-right: 1em;"><img alt="" data-original-height="3988" data-original-width="3000" height="640" src="/images/0844dd4f3ce31df8.jpg=w483-h640" width="483" /></a></div><br />&nbsp;<div class="separator" style="clear: both; text-align: center;"><a href="/images/836788df0fb9811f.jpg" style="margin-left: 1em; margin-right: 1em;"><img alt="" data-original-height="2700" data-original-width="3591" height="301" src="/images/836788df0fb9811f.jpg" width="400" />&nbsp;</a>&nbsp;</div><div class="separator" style="clear: both; text-align: center;"><a href="/images/0f42323a94acc441.jpg" style="margin-left: 1em; margin-right: 1em;"><img alt="" data-original-height="3549" data-original-width="2668" height="640" src="/images/0f42323a94acc441.jpg=w480-h640" width="480" /></a></div><br /><a href="https://www.strava.com/activities/16458810542">108 km / 67 mi</a><p></p>

<h2 style="text-align: left;">Ride 7: Barriques in Middleton</h2><p>My buddy Ben had sold me on a new Zwift race series, schedule at 7:45 am on Sundays. The race was long and extremely hard. But it was also another uncharacteristically warm and sunny day and so Nicole and I headed out to Middleton for the last Coffeeneuring ride. Another destination that I'd been too many times: Barriques. While I don't like their filtered coffees, the espresso is reliably decent.&nbsp;</p><p></p><div class="separator" style="clear: both; text-align: center;"><a href="/images/a76daa112aa412c8.jpg" style="margin-left: 1em; margin-right: 1em;"><img alt="" data-original-height="3648" data-original-width="2736" height="640" src="/images/a76daa112aa412c8.jpg=w480-h640" width="480" /></a></div><br /><div class="separator" style="clear: both; text-align: center;"><a href="/images/d08556a7cca11307.jpg" style="margin-left: 1em; margin-right: 1em;"><img alt="" data-original-height="4064" data-original-width="3056" height="640" src="/images/d08556a7cca11307.jpg" width="480" /></a></div><br /><div class="separator" style="clear: both; text-align: center;"><a href="/images/4e18d7067beeb5f7.jpg" style="margin-left: 1em; margin-right: 1em;"><img alt="" data-original-height="3056" data-original-width="3056" height="640" src="/images/4e18d7067beeb5f7.jpg" width="640" /></a></div><br />&nbsp;<a href="https://www.strava.com/activities/16547155782">26 km / 16 mi</a><p></p>
