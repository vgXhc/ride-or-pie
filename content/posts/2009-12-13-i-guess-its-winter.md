We had quite a bit of snow during the last couple of days. This was a strong encouragement for me to finally replace Wolfgang's 700x28c Conti Gatorskin slicks with my 700x32c 'cross tires. I put them on yesterday (front) and today (back) and now should be ready for the coming snows of this season.<br />
<br />
A related side note: This summer I had ordered the <a href="http://www.crankbrothers.com/speedlever.php" tooltip="linkalert-tip">Crank Brothers Speed Lever</a>. I tend to misplace traditional tire levers and my disappointing experience with the <a href="http://www.californiabikegear.com/quikstik/public/home.php" tooltip="linkalert-tip">Quik Stik</a> led me to test a new solution. I must say that I'm really happy with the Speed Lever. Only one piece instead of three and it does indeed work fast. The downside: for really tight tire-rim combinations you'll still need an additional lever to get the tire on and off. <br />
