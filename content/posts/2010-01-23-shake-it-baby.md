Short ride report for a short ride.<br />
<br />

<br />
I had to go up to campus to get a couple of books and I didn't feel like taking the direct route. So I went up South Hill and hit the trailhead for the <a href="http://www.town.ithaca.ny.us/parks/southhilldrecreationway.htm">South Hill Recreation Way</a>. I was worried that it might be all ice, but actually it was a very rough, frozen snow surface. Traction was okay (I couldn't go much faster than 10 km/h anyway) but it was as bumpy as a washboard. I had never taken the trail before and it was completely dark, and thus I missed an intersection and was led back to Coddington Road instead of taking the trail all the way up to Burns Road.<br />
<br />
Notable events: I used my <a href="http://www.heatmax.com/HotHands/toasietoes.htm">Toasti Toes foot warmers</a> in my normal cycling shoes for the first time. Result: Numb feet after the uphill part, but got back to normal much faster than usual. And on the short descent into Ithaca they kept my feet surprisingly warm.
