It was a dark and stormy night. Okay, it wasn't quite dark yet, and the wind was strong but didn't quite qualify as "stormy." After a slow Sunday I overcame the urge to just spend the rest of the day on the couch and went on another <a href="https://chasingmailboxes.com/2017/09/27/seven-is-a-lucky-number-the-7th-annual-coffeeneuring-challenge/">coffeeneuring</a> excursion. My destination was <a href="http://dnr.wi.gov/topic/parks/name/lakekegonsa/">Lake Kegonsa State Park</a>, via Lower Yahara River Trail.<br />
